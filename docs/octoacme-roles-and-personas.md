# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment windows, and rollback planning so that delivery activities are predictable and low-risk.

### Responsibilities
- Own release schedules and readiness reviews
- Coordinate deployment plans with engineering, QA, and stakeholders
- Confirm that release notes, rollback plans, and support communication are complete
- Track post-release health and escalate issues when production impact occurs
- Ensure operational readiness before go-live

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and production disruption
- Improve predictability across milestones and deployments
- Align engineering, support, and stakeholder expectations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness meetings and deployment check-ins
- Post-release summaries and incident follow-ups
- Status updates to PMs, Product Leads, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with roadmap commitments
- Partners with Developers and QA to validate deployment readiness and smoke tests
- Coordinates with Product Managers on stakeholder communications and launch plans
- Escalates issues to leadership when deployment risk or business impact requires intervention

---

## Security & Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads ensure that projects meet required safeguards, policy standards, and risk controls throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs for security and compliance considerations
- Identify vulnerabilities, access risks, and data-handling concerns
- Partner with engineering to define mitigations and remediation timelines
- Ensure release readiness aligns with security policies and approvals
- Support incident response and post-incident reviews when needed

### Goals
- Reduce security risk and operational exposure
- Maintain policy compliance without slowing delivery unnecessarily
- Build trust through transparent risk management

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and threat assessments
- Security check-ins during planning and release gates
- Escalation notices for critical findings or incidents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying risk severity and required mitigation steps
- Works with Developers to review technical implementation and controls
- Advises Product Managers on customer and business risk, especially for data handling or regulatory concerns
- Provides decision support for release timing and rollback decisions when security issues arise

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect delivery work to customer experience, support readiness, and operational feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, feature requests, and pain points
- Highlight adoption, support, or escalations that may affect delivery priorities
- Coordinate readiness for rollout, training, and support coverage
- Share trends from support channels that should influence product decisions
- Help align stakeholder expectations with delivery reality

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and successful adoption
- Surface issues early before they become churn or escalation risks
- Improve the link between product delivery and customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and support trend reports
- Stakeholder updates tied to launch readiness or adoption impacts
- Escalation and issue triage with Product and Project teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds market and customer realities into Product Manager prioritization
- Works with Project Managers on launch timing, readiness, and communication plans
- Provides Developers and QA with issue context and usage patterns that affect release quality
- Helps Product and PM teams prepare stakeholder messaging based on customer impact

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads provide measurable evidence for delivery decisions, success tracking, and operational learning.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation needed to evaluate outcomes
- Monitor release and adoption metrics against agreed success criteria
- Support experimentation, validation, and reporting for product decisions
- Help teams interpret data quality issues and trends
- Translate performance data into actionable recommendations for stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with evidence and clear metrics
- Measure progress against business and customer outcomes
- Identify opportunities to optimize product value and delivery performance

### Typical Communication
- KPI dashboards and weekly performance reviews
- Post-launch impact reports and milestone health checks
- Data-informed recommendations for product and project decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers validate whether outcomes are meeting stated goals
- Supports Project Managers with milestone health, risk indicators, and reporting
- Provides Developers with meaningful feedback on product performance and quality metrics
- Partners with Stakeholders to explain impact and trade-offs based on measurable outcomes

---

## Technical Architects / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Engineering Leads keep the solution aligned with technical direction, quality standards, and cross-team dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Define architectural direction, key technical decisions, and integration patterns
- Identify dependencies, risks, and constraints across systems or teams
- Guide design reviews and ensure implementation quality remains aligned to the target architecture
- Support capacity planning and sequencing of technical work
- Translate business priorities into technical execution strategies

### Goals
- Balance speed with maintainability and delivery quality
- Reduce architectural debt and cross-team rework
- Improve execution clarity across complex or interdependent systems

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical decision logs
- Dependency and sequencing discussions with delivery teams
- Design recommendations and engineering risk updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers assess feasibility and trade-offs for roadmap items
- Collaborates closely with Developers to maintain implementation quality and consistency
- Works with Project Managers to manage sequencing, risk, and delivery dependencies
- Supports Security Leads on design review and control implementation when needed

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify accountability, decision ownership, and collaboration patterns across cross-functional projects.
